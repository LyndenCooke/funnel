AI Training That Actually Changes Practice

Run AI is not another CPD provider selling a one-day session. It is a free, ongoing community where your teachers develop AI skills at their own pace, supported by a structured framework and peer learning.

Structured Progression

The Run AI level-up system gives you a clear framework for staff development. From AI Explorer to AI Native, each level has defined skills and milestones. You can track where each team member is and set targets for the term.

AI Readiness Audit

Start by benchmarking your team. The AI Readiness Audit takes 2 minutes per teacher and gives each person a personalised score and level. Use the results to identify training priorities and group staff by ability.

Zero Budget Required

The Run AI community is completely free. No procurement process. No budget approval. Teachers join individually and learn at their own pace. For schools wanting structured sessions, contact Lynden directly.
