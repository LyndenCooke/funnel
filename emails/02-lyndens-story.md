Hey {{ subscriber.first_name }},

I want to tell you why I built Run AI. Not the polished version. The real one.

I am a UK-qualified primary teacher, currently based in Qatar. I have taught every year group from Reception to Year 6, specialising in British curriculum, EAL, and SEN. I know what it is like to spend your Sunday evening buried in planning.

About a year ago, I started playing with ChatGPT. Not because I am a tech person. Because I was tired and looking for a shortcut.

It was messy at first. The outputs were rubbish. I did not know how to write a decent prompt. I nearly gave up more than once.

But I kept experimenting. I documented what worked and what did not. I started sharing it on LinkedIn. Other teachers noticed. They started asking questions. The audience grew to over 1,000 people, mostly teachers just like you who wanted practical guidance without the jargon.

That is when I realised something. The problem was not that teachers did not want to use AI. The problem was that nobody was showing them how in a way that made sense for their actual day.

So I built Run AI. Not as an expert handing down wisdom from above. As a working teacher, one step ahead, holding the door open.

I still get things wrong. I still experiment in public. And when I find something that saves time or makes a lesson better, I share it with this community first.

I would love to know your story too. Head into the community and introduce yourself. Tell us what you teach, where you are, and what brought you here.
