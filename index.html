Step 1: Copy this prompt. "Create a 5-lesson plan for [Year Group] [Subject] on [Topic]. Each lesson should be [duration], include a starter activity, main teaching input, practical activity, and plenary. Differentiate for three ability groups. Align with the National Curriculum for England." Step 2: Fill in the blanks. For example: Year 4, Maths, Fractions, 60 minutes. Step 3: Paste it into ChatGPT at chat.openai.com.
