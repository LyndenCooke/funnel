# Run AI — Positioning Document

## The Gap

The AI-for-teachers space is crowded with tools (MagicSchool, Eduaide, TeacherMatic) and training providers (Century Tech, Oak AI). But there is a consistent gap that none of them fill:

**Nobody is building in public as a working teacher.**

Every existing option falls into one of two categories:

1. **Tool vendors** who package AI behind a teacher-friendly interface. The teacher never learns how AI actually works. They become dependent on a subscription rather than developing transferable skills.

2. **Training providers** who deliver polished CPD sessions full of theory, frameworks, and jargon. The training is delivered by consultants, not classroom teachers. It is abstract, not practical. Teachers leave with slides, not skills.

Run AI sits in the gap between these two: a community led by a working teacher who is documenting his own AI learning journey in real time, showing the messy real process, and teaching at the level he is actually at. Not polished tutorials. Not guru posturing. Just a teacher showing other teachers how to actually use these tools, step by step, with real classroom examples.

## The Enemy

Run AI positions itself against three things:

1. **Generic AI hype.** The constant noise about AI replacing teachers, transforming education overnight, and solving every problem. This creates anxiety without action. Teachers hear "AI will change everything" but nobody shows them what to do on Monday morning.

2. **Overpriced CPD that teaches theory, not practice.** Schools spend thousands on AI training that amounts to a consultant showing slides about what AI could do, without ever opening a tool and building something. Teachers leave these sessions more confused than when they arrived.

3. **Tools that promise magic but require no skill development.** Platforms like MagicSchool wrap AI in a simple interface and say "just click here." The teacher never learns prompting, never understands how AI thinks, never develops the transferable skills to use AI independently. When the tool changes or the subscription ends, they are back to square one.

The anti-positioning is clear: **Run AI is the anti-course.** It is not something you watch. It is something you do.

## The Promise

**"Go from AI-curious to AI-native in 90 days — with a community of teachers doing it alongside you."**

This promise is specific (90 days, not "eventually"), achievable (AI-native is defined through clear levels), social (a community, not a solo course), and credible (the guide is a teacher, not a consultant).

What "AI-native" means: AI is embedded in your daily practice. You do not think about whether to use AI. You think about how to use AI. It has become as natural as using a search engine or a word processor.

## The Proof Mechanism

The proof is Lynden's own journey:

- A UK-qualified primary teacher who taught Reception through Year 6
- Got curious about AI, started experimenting, started building
- Documented everything publicly on LinkedIn
- Built real AI tools for lesson planning and assessment
- Grew a following of 1,000+ educators watching and learning from the process
- Transitioned from consumer to builder, and now teaches others to do the same

The key credibility marker: **he is not ahead of the audience by years. He is ahead by months.** He remembers what it was like to be confused, sceptical, and overwhelmed. He teaches at the level he is actually at, not the level he wishes he was at.

This is more relatable and trustworthy than an AI consultant who has never planned a lesson or written a report card.

## The Differentiation

Five things that make Run AI structurally different from everything else in the market:

### 1. Gamified Progression System (Levels)
Five clearly defined levels from AI Explorer to AI Native. Each level has specific skills, challenges, and milestones. Teachers always know where they are, what they need to learn next, and how far they have come. This is motivating, trackable, and shareable.

### 2. Build-Along Projects, Not Passive Video Watching
Every week, Lynden builds something live. A prompt library. A workflow. A custom tool. Community members build alongside him. Learning happens through doing, not watching.

### 3. Visual Learning Through Interactive Boards
Complex AI concepts are mapped visually using tools like Figma. Teachers can see how tools connect, how workflows chain together, and how their skills build on each other. This suits visual learners and makes abstract concepts tangible.

### 4. Community-First Model
Run AI is a Skool community, not a course platform. The value comes from peer support, shared resources, and collective problem-solving. Teachers help teachers. The community grows the resource library organically.

### 5. Anti-Guru Positioning
Lynden does not position himself as an expert. He positions himself as a guide who is one step ahead. This removes the intimidation barrier that stops many teachers from engaging with AI training. If he can do it, they can do it.

## Target Audience

**Primary audience:** UK primary and secondary teachers aged 28 to 45 who are AI-curious but feel overwhelmed, sceptical, or stuck.

**Secondary audience:** CPD coordinators and school leaders looking for practical AI training that their staff will actually use.

**Tertiary audience:** Education consultants and international school teachers (particularly those following the British curriculum) who want to add AI skills to their offering.

**Psychographic profile:** These are competent, hardworking professionals who care about their students and their craft. They are not technophobes. They use technology daily. But AI feels different. It feels risky, complex, and hyped. They want someone they trust to show them it is manageable, practical, and genuinely useful.

## Pricing Strategy

- **Free tier (Skool community):** Full access to the community, level-up framework, weekly builds, and resource library. This is the growth engine. No barriers to entry.
- **Premium tier (£20/month, future):** Advanced workshops, personalised feedback, early access to tools Lynden builds, and a monthly live build session. Introduced once the free community hits critical mass (500+ members).

The free tier must be genuinely valuable. The premium tier adds speed and depth, not access to the core experience.
