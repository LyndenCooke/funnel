# SEO Keyword Research for Run AI

**Date:** February 2026
**Purpose:** Identify 10 target keywords/topics for SEO content pages, ranked by estimated opportunity.

---

## Research Method

Based on teacher pain points from competitor analysis, UK education search behaviour patterns, and long-tail keyword logic for a new site with low domain authority. Prioritising terms where:
- Search intent matches what Run AI offers (training/community, not just tools)
- Competition is low enough for a new site to rank
- The searcher is likely a UK teacher (British curriculum terms, UK-specific phrasing)
- Content can naturally lead to a community signup or audit CTA

---

## Top 10 Keywords Ranked by Opportunity

### 1. "AI tools for primary teachers"
- **Intent:** Discovery. Teachers looking for practical tools they can use.
- **Competition:** Low-medium. Most results are listicle blog posts, not dedicated pages.
- **Opportunity:** High. Run AI can rank with a genuinely useful guide written by a primary teacher, not a tech reviewer.
- **Content angle:** Curated list of free and affordable AI tools with real classroom examples from KS1/KS2.

### 2. "how to use AI for lesson planning"
- **Intent:** Practical how-to. Teachers who want to start but do not know the steps.
- **Competition:** Low. Most results are generic articles or tool vendor pages.
- **Opportunity:** High. Step-by-step guide with real prompts and examples beats generic advice.
- **Content angle:** A practical walkthrough showing how to plan a week of lessons using AI, with actual prompt templates.

### 3. "AI CPD for schools UK"
- **Intent:** Research/procurement. CPD coordinators and school leaders looking for training options.
- **Competition:** Low. Few dedicated pages target this exact phrase.
- **Opportunity:** High. Decision-makers searching this term have budget and authority.
- **Content angle:** What good AI CPD looks like vs. what most schools get. Position Run AI community as ongoing CPD, not a one-off session.

### 4. "free AI tools for teachers UK"
- **Intent:** Discovery with price filter. Teachers on tight budgets (most of them).
- **Competition:** Medium. Several listicle articles exist but most are US-focused.
- **Opportunity:** Medium-high. UK-specific focus is the differentiator.
- **Content angle:** UK-focused list including Oak AI (Aila), free tiers of MagicSchool/Eduaide, and general tools like ChatGPT with teacher-specific prompts.

### 5. "ChatGPT for teachers UK"
- **Intent:** Specific tool exploration. Teachers who have heard of ChatGPT and want to use it.
- **Competition:** Medium. Some good articles exist but many are generic.
- **Opportunity:** Medium-high. Brand name in the query drives volume.
- **Content angle:** Practical guide to using ChatGPT specifically for UK curriculum tasks (KS1-KS4), with prompt templates.

### 6. "AI lesson planning KS2"
- **Intent:** Very specific. Primary teachers looking for KS2 planning help.
- **Competition:** Low. Highly specific long-tail with few dedicated pages.
- **Opportunity:** Medium. Lower volume but extremely targeted.
- **Content angle:** Detailed guide with subject-specific examples for Year 3-6 teachers.

### 7. "how to use AI for marking and assessment"
- **Intent:** Task-specific. Teachers looking to reduce marking workload.
- **Competition:** Low-medium. Most content is about AI grading tools, not practical teacher workflows.
- **Opportunity:** Medium. Marking is the top time-sink teachers want to solve.
- **Content angle:** Practical workflows for using AI to speed up feedback, report writing, and assessment design (not automated grading).

### 8. "AI in the classroom for beginners"
- **Intent:** Beginner exploration. Teachers at the very start of their AI journey.
- **Competition:** Low. Generic results, few targeted pages.
- **Opportunity:** Medium. Good top-of-funnel content for the AI Explorer level.
- **Content angle:** A beginner-friendly guide that addresses fears and scepticism before showing practical first steps.

### 9. "AI policy for schools UK"
- **Intent:** Policy research. School leaders and CPD coordinators.
- **Competition:** Low-medium. DfE guidance exists but practical implementation guides are rare.
- **Opportunity:** Medium. Captures decision-makers searching for policy frameworks.
- **Content angle:** Practical guide to writing an AI acceptable use policy, with a template schools can adapt.

### 10. "best AI tools for teachers 2026"
- **Intent:** Annual roundup search. Teachers looking for current recommendations.
- **Competition:** Medium-high. Many publications target "[year]" roundups.
- **Opportunity:** Lower for a new site, but the content has natural link-building potential.
- **Content angle:** Honest, opinionated roundup from a working teacher, not a sponsored listicle.

---

## Selected for Content Pages

Based on the ranking above, the top 3 keywords for dedicated SEO pages are:

1. **"AI tools for primary teachers"** — Highest opportunity, matches Run AI's primary teacher positioning.
2. **"how to use AI for lesson planning"** — Practical intent, high conversion potential.
3. **"AI CPD for schools UK"** — Captures decision-makers, positions Run AI as ongoing CPD.
