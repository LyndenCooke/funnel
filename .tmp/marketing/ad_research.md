# LinkedIn Ad Research for Run AI

**Date:** February 2026
**Purpose:** Inform static ad creative for LinkedIn campaigns targeting UK teachers.

---

## 1. What Ad Formats Perform Best on LinkedIn

### Single Image Ads
- The workhorse format. Accounts for the majority of LinkedIn ad spend.
- Best for awareness and lead generation with a clear, single message.
- Recommended dimensions: 1200x627px (landscape) or 1080x1080px (square).
- Square images take up more mobile feed space, which improves stopping power.

### Carousel Ads
- Multiple cards (2-10) that users swipe through.
- Strong for storytelling and multi-step narratives.
- Higher engagement rates than single image when the story justifies the format.
- Risk: if the first card does not hook, users will not swipe.

### Document Ads
- Upload a PDF that users can scroll through in-feed.
- Excellent for thought leadership and lead magnets.
- Can gate the document behind a lead form after a few pages.
- Works well for frameworks, checklists, and guides.

### Video Ads
- Autoplay in-feed (muted). Must work without sound.
- Best for brand awareness and emotional storytelling.
- Recommended: 15-30 seconds for feed, under 15 seconds for maximum completion rates.

### For Run AI: Single image and carousel are the priority. Document ads could work for the AI Readiness Audit as a lead magnet. Video is a secondary format.

## 2. Visual Styles That Work in Education

### What gets engagement in the education space:
- **Warm, approachable colour palettes.** Earth tones, warm neutrals, soft blues. Not the neon gradients and dark backgrounds of tech advertising.
- **Real photography over stock.** Teachers respond to images that look like actual classrooms, not corporate stock photos. Since we do not have photography, illustrated or typographic approaches work better than generic stock.
- **Clean typography as the hero element.** When you do not have photography, bold text-based designs with strong hierarchy outperform cluttered layouts.
- **Human-scale messaging.** Education audiences respond to specificity ("Year 4 teachers") over broad claims ("all educators").
- **No Silicon Valley aesthetic.** No dark mode, no gradients, no "AI brain" illustrations, no robot imagery. These trigger scepticism in education audiences.

### What competitors are running:
- **MagicSchool:** Clean, product-focused ads showing the tool interface. Purple/blue brand colours. Focus on "save time" messaging.
- **Eduaide:** Minimal ads, more organic content. When they advertise, it is tool-specific with screenshots.
- **TeacherMatic:** UK-focused, straightforward messaging about saving time. Uses their green brand colour.
- **Oak AI (Aila):** Government-backed, institutional feel. Clean, accessible design. Free positioning is the hook.

### Run AI opportunity:
- None of the competitors use a warm, human, community-first visual identity.
- The navy/cream/terracotta palette is distinctive in a space dominated by blues, purples, and greens.
- Text-based ads with strong hooks will stand out against tool-screenshot-heavy competitor ads.

## 3. Hooks and Angles for Free Community Offers

### What works for free community/group offers on LinkedIn:

**Pain-led hooks:**
- Lead with a specific frustration the audience recognises immediately.
- "You spent Sunday afternoon planning lessons AI could draft in minutes."
- The hook must be visible before the "see more" fold (roughly 2 lines on mobile).

**Identity hooks:**
- Position the reader as a specific type of person.
- "For teachers who know AI matters but have no idea where to start."
- Creates self-selection: the right audience thinks "that is me."

**Social proof hooks:**
- Specific numbers over vague claims.
- "100+ teachers are saving 10+ hours a week" is stronger than "join our community."
- Odd numbers and non-round figures feel more credible.

**Objection-reversal hooks:**
- Address the primary reason someone would not click.
- "You do not need to be technical" directly counters the biggest barrier.

**Curiosity/quiz hooks:**
- Interactive elements drive higher CTR on LinkedIn.
- "What is your AI Readiness Score?" creates an open loop the reader wants to close.

### CTA best practices:
- "Join Free" outperforms "Learn More" for community offers.
- Adding a benefit to the CTA: "Join Free and Save 10 Hours a Week."
- Urgency should be real, not manufactured. "Free while we grow" is honest.

## 4. Competitor Ad Analysis

### MagicSchool AI
- Runs consistent LinkedIn sponsored content.
- Messaging centres on "save time" and "80+ tools."
- Visual style: product screenshots, blue/purple gradients, professional but corporate.
- Weakness: feels like a SaaS product, not a community. Teachers are a demographic to them, not peers.

### Eduaide
- Limited paid advertising. Relies on organic content from co-founder Thomas Hummel.
- When they do run ads, focus on the pedagogical approach.
- Weakness: low brand awareness outside early adopters.

### TeacherMatic
- UK-focused ads, particularly around back-to-school periods.
- Messaging: "AI generators for teachers." Straightforward tool positioning.
- Weakness: feels like a utility, not a movement.

### Oak AI (Aila)
- Government-backed, so promotion comes through institutional channels (DfE, school networks).
- Free positioning is compelling but feels institutional, not personal.
- Weakness: no community element. It is a tool, not a journey.

### Run AI differentiation in ads:
- **Human voice** where competitors sound corporate or institutional.
- **Community** where competitors sell tools.
- **Journey** where competitors sell features.
- **Warmth** where competitors default to tech aesthetics.

## 5. Key Decisions for Ad Creative

1. **Lead with text, not imagery.** Our strongest asset is the copy. The hook line should dominate the visual.
2. **Use the brand palette consistently.** Navy backgrounds with cream text, or cream backgrounds with navy text. Terracotta for CTAs and accents.
3. **One message per ad.** Do not try to communicate community + audit + levels in a single frame.
4. **Mobile-first design.** Square (1080x1080) format should be the primary design, landscape (1200x627) secondary.
5. **Include "Run AI Community" as text.** No logo file exists, so the wordmark in DM Serif Display serves as the brand mark.
